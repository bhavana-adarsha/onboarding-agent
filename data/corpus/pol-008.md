# Benefits Enrollment Overview and Deadlines

## Annual Enrollment Period

Meridian Health Partners conducts our annual benefits enrollment each fall. **2024 Enrollment Window: October 14 – October 28**. All eligible employees must complete enrollment during this window. Changes made outside this period will not take effect until the following plan year (January 1, 2025).

New hires have a separate 30-day enrollment window beginning on their first day of employment.

## Eligible Employees

Full-time employees (30+ hours weekly) are eligible for benefits on the first of the month following 30 days of employment. Part-time employees working 20–29 hours weekly may enroll in medical and dental plans only. Contractors and temporary staff are not eligible.

## Available Plans

**Medical Insurance**
- Meridian Blue PPO (in-network: $500 individual deductible)
- Meridian Blue HDHP with HSA ($1,500 individual deductible; company contributes $500 annually to HSA)
- Meridian Care HMO (limited to employees in metro service areas)

**Dental Insurance**
- Standard Plan (80% coverage after deductible)
- Premium Plan (90% coverage; $50 higher monthly premium)

**Vision Insurance**
- One plan available; covers annual eye exam and $150 toward frames/contacts

**Supplemental Coverage**
- Life Insurance: 1x or 2x salary options (company pays 1x baseline)
- Short-term Disability: 60% wage replacement, 14-day waiting period
- Long-term Disability: 60% wage replacement after 90 days

**Flexible Spending Accounts (FSA)**
- Healthcare FSA: up to $3,200 annually (2024 limit)
- Dependent Care FSA: up to $5,000 annually

## Enrollment Process

1. Log into Workday (workday.meridianhealth.com)
2. Navigate to "Benefits" → "Enroll Now"
3. Review plan options and coverage details in the embedded decision support tool
4. Select medical, dental, and vision elections
5. Confirm beneficiaries and dependent information
6. Submit enrollment by 11:59 PM PT on October 28

HR will send reminder emails on October 14, 21, and 27. Questions? Contact the HR Service Center at hr@meridianhealth.com or ext. 5500.

## Important Notes

- **Qualifying Life Events**: Marriage, divorce, birth/adoption, loss of coverage, or relocation may allow mid-year changes. Submit documentation to HR within 30 days.
- **Rates**: 2024 monthly premiums are posted on the HR portal. Employee contributions are pre-tax (payroll deduction).
- **Dependent Documentation**: Spouses and children require proof of relationship (marriage certificate, birth certificate). Domestic partners must complete affidavit form (available from HR).
- **Coverage Effective Date**: Elections submitted by October 28 become effective January 1, 2025.

Previous enrollment periods: October 2023 (Oct 9–23), October 2022 (Oct 10–24). We moved the 2024 window one week earlier due to payroll system upgrades in early November.
