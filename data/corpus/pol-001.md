# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

## Overview

Meridian Health Partners provides all full-time employees with 15 days of paid time off annually. This policy applies to all employees in Claims Ops, Care Coordination, Data & Analytics, Platform Engineering, and HR across all office locations.

## Accrual and Year Definition

PTO accrues on a calendar year basis (January 1 – December 31). Employees receive their full 15-day allocation on January 1st each year. This applies to new hires as well, prorated to their start date.

**Example:** An employee hired on July 15th receives 7.5 days of PTO for that calendar year (50% of 15 days).

## Requesting PTO

All PTO requests must be submitted through Workday at least 14 days in advance. Requests are submitted to your direct manager for approval.

**Process:**
1. Open Workday and navigate to Time Off
2. Select "PTO Request"
3. Choose dates and enter reason (optional)
4. Submit for manager approval
5. You will receive email confirmation once approved

For urgent requests with less than 14 days' notice, email your manager directly and follow up in Workday.

## Blackout Dates and Restrictions

Certain periods have restricted PTO availability due to business needs:

- **Month-end close (last 5 business days of each month):** Claims Ops team members cannot take PTO
- **Q4 planning period (October 1-15):** Data & Analytics team members cannot take PTO
- **Holiday weeks:** Thanksgiving week and the week of December 24-28 require manager approval and are limited to 2 employees per team per day

## Use-It-or-Lose-It Policy

Unused PTO does not carry over to the next calendar year. All 15 days must be used by December 31st or they are forfeited. Managers are responsible for encouraging their teams to use PTO throughout the year.

## Holidays

Meridian Health Partners observes 10 company holidays annually (separate from PTO). These include New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Thanksgiving, and Christmas. Holiday schedules are posted in Confluence by November 1st each year.

## PTO During Separation

If an employee leaves Meridian Health Partners, unused PTO is not paid out except where required by state law. Employees should coordinate their final PTO dates with their manager and HR during the offboarding process.

## Medical Leave

Extended medical leave beyond 15 days of PTO should be discussed with HR. Short-term disability and other leave options may be available. Contact the HR team in Slack channel #hr-support.

## Questions and Updates

Questions about PTO should be directed to your manager or HR. This policy is reviewed annually and updates will be posted in Confluence. The last update to this policy was April 2023.
