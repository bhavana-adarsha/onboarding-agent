# Requesting a Dataset from Data & Analytics

## Overview

Data & Analytics maintains the enterprise data warehouse and produces custom datasets for operational teams. This runbook covers the process for requesting a new dataset or report.

## Before You Request

Check if what you need already exists:

- Search **Confluence** for "Data Catalog" (updated quarterly, sometimes behind)
- Ask your team lead if someone has built this before
- Review existing PulseBoard dashboards—your data might already be there
- Check the #data-analytics Slack channel for recent requests

## Step 1: Create a Jira Ticket

1. Go to **Jira** and create a new issue in the **DAT** (Data & Analytics) project
2. Use the **"Data Request"** issue type
3. Fill in required fields:
   - **Summary**: Be specific. Good: "Weekly claims denial rate by denial code and region." Bad: "I need data."
   - **Description**: Include business context and how you'll use the data
   - **Requestor Team**: Select from dropdown (Claims Ops, Care Coordination, etc.)
   - **Data Sources**: Specify which systems (ClaimsFlow, CareBridge, Workday, etc.)
   - **Timeline**: Select "Routine" (5-7 business days), "Urgent" (2-3 business days), or "Blocking" (next business day)
   - **Output Format**: CSV, Excel, PulseBoard dashboard, or automated Slack report

## Step 2: Provide Details

Include specifics to avoid back-and-forth:

- **Date range**: "Last 12 months" or "rolling 90 days"?
- **Granularity**: Daily? Weekly? By provider? By region?
- **Filters**: Only active claims? Exclude test records? Specific product lines?
- **Metrics needed**: List exact calculations (e.g., "denial rate = denied claims / total claims submitted")
- **Sample output**: Attach a mockup or screenshot of what you're imagining

## Step 3: Add to Confluence

Link your Jira ticket in the **Data Requests** Confluence page under your team section. This helps track institutional knowledge.

## Step 4: Wait for Prioritization

Data & Analytics reviews new requests every Monday and Thursday. They'll:

- Comment on your ticket with questions (usually within 1 business day)
- Assign an owner from the DAT team
- Move ticket to "In Progress" when work begins

Response time depends on complexity and current workload. Blocking requests get priority but should be rare.

## Step 5: Receive Your Dataset

Once complete, Data & Analytics will:

- Attach the file to the Jira ticket, or
- Create a PulseBoard dashboard and share the link, or
- Set up an automated Slack report to your channel

They'll notify you in the ticket comments and via Slack.

## Common Mistakes

- **Vague requests**: "I need claims data" won't work. Be specific.
- **Wrong timeline**: Don't mark everything "Blocking." Reserve for genuine emergencies.
- **Missing context**: Tell us *why* you need this. It helps us suggest alternatives.
- **Scope creep**: Submit new requests instead of continuously modifying old ones.
