# TimeTrax Timesheet Submission & Approval Runbook

## Overview

All non-exempt employees at Meridian Health Partners must submit timesheets weekly via TimeTrax. Timesheets are due by **5 PM ET every Friday** for the preceding Sunday–Saturday work week. Managers must approve timesheets by **EOD Monday** of the following week.

## For Employees: Submitting Your Timesheet

### Step 1: Log In
Navigate to `timesheets.meridian.local` and authenticate using your Meridian credentials (same username/password as Slack and Workday).

### Step 2: Verify Your Schedule
Click **"My Week"** in the left sidebar. TimeTrax should display your scheduled shifts based on data synced from your manager's calendar. Review for accuracy.

- **Note:** Scheduled shifts appear in light blue. If something is missing, contact your manager before submitting.

### Step 3: Record Hours
For each day worked, enter actual hours in the **"Hours Worked"** column. This may differ from scheduled hours if you left early, came in late, or worked overtime.

- Example: If you were scheduled 8 hours but left 30 minutes early for a medical appointment, enter 7.5 hours.

### Step 4: Add Notes (Optional but Recommended)
Use the **"Notes"** field to flag anything unusual: extended breaks, overtime, or schedule changes approved by your manager.

### Step 5: Submit
Click the **"Submit Timesheet"** button at the bottom right. You'll see a confirmation message: *"Timesheet submitted for week ending [DATE]."* You'll also receive an email confirmation.

- **Important:** Once submitted, you cannot edit. Contact your manager if corrections are needed.

## For Managers: Approving Timesheets

### Step 1: Access Team Timesheets
Log in to TimeTrax and select **"Team Timesheets"** from the dashboard. You'll see all direct reports' submitted timesheets.

### Step 2: Review Each Submission
Click on an employee's name to view their detailed timesheet. Check:
- Total hours match expected weekly hours (typically 40 for full-time)
- No unusual patterns or gaps
- Notes explain any deviations

### Step 3: Verify Against Schedules
Cross-reference the submitted hours against the schedule you set in TimeTrax. Contact the employee if discrepancies exist.

### Step 4: Approve or Reject
- **To Approve:** Click the green **"Approve"** button. The timesheet moves to Finance.
- **To Reject:** Click **"Request Changes,"** add a comment explaining what needs correction, and click **"Send Back."** The employee will receive a notification and can resubmit.

### Step 5: Confirm Submission to Payroll
Once you've approved all team timesheets, click **"Submit to Payroll"** by EOD Monday. This locks the week and prevents further edits.

## Escalations

If you encounter technical issues, post in the **#timesheets-support** Slack channel. The Platform Engineering team monitors this daily.

For timesheet disputes or policy questions, contact HR at `hr-support@meridian.local`.

## Key Deadlines

| Action | Deadline |
|--------|----------|
| Employee submission | Friday 5 PM ET |
| Manager approval | Monday 5 PM ET |
| Payroll lock | Monday EOD |
