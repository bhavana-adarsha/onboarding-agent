# Code of Conduct Summary

## Purpose

This document outlines the core behavioral expectations for all Meridian Health Partners employees across all departments and locations. Our Code of Conduct supports our mission to improve healthcare outcomes while maintaining a respectful, professional workplace.

## Core Principles

All employees must adhere to the following principles:

- **Integrity**: Conduct business honestly and transparently in all interactions
- **Respect**: Treat colleagues, clients, and partners with dignity regardless of role or background
- **Compliance**: Follow all applicable healthcare regulations, company policies, and legal requirements
- **Accountability**: Take responsibility for your actions and decisions

## Professional Conduct Standards

### Workplace Behavior

- Maintain professional communication across all platforms (Slack, email, in-person meetings)
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, or hostile behavior based on protected characteristics
- Report concerns through your direct manager or HR (hr@meridianhealth.com)
- Dress code: Business casual in office settings; follow department guidelines for client meetings

### Confidentiality and Data Protection

- Never share patient health information (PHI) outside authorized channels
- All ClaimsFlow, CareBridge, and PulseBoard data must remain confidential
- Use secure passwords and enable two-factor authentication on all company systems
- Report suspected data breaches to Platform Engineering immediately (security@meridianhealth.com)
- Violations may result in termination and legal action

### Conflicts of Interest

- Disclose any financial interests, family relationships, or personal connections to colleagues or clients
- Do not solicit personal favors from business partners or vendors
- Avoid accepting gifts valued over $50 from external parties
- Report conflicts to your department head within 5 business days

## Social Media and External Communications

- Do not identify Meridian Health Partners clients or patient outcomes on personal social media
- Company social accounts are managed exclusively by Marketing
- Employees speaking publicly about company matters must receive written approval from Communications
- Personal opinions on social media should not represent company positions

## System and Tool Usage

- ClaimsFlow, CareBridge, TimeTrax, and all company systems are for business purposes only
- Limit personal use of company devices and Slack to minimal, reasonable breaks
- Do not attempt to access systems outside your authorized role or department
- GitHub commits must include meaningful messages and reference corresponding Jira tickets

## Violations and Consequences

Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in:

- Verbal warning (first minor offense)
- Written warning (second offense or serious first offense)
- Suspension (repeated violations)
- Termination (severe violations including fraud, data theft, or harassment)

All employees must complete Code of Conduct training annually. New hires complete training during onboarding with HR.

## Questions?

Contact Human Resources at hr@meridianhealth.com or visit the Code of Conduct page on Confluence (internal-policies/code-of-conduct).
