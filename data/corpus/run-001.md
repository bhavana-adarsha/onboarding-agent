# ClaimsFlow Standard Claim Processing Runbook

## Overview

This runbook covers the standard workflow for processing a routine medical claim in ClaimsFlow. Most claims follow this path; complex cases requiring manual review are handled separately (see "Escalation Procedures" in Confluence).

## Prerequisites

- Active ClaimsFlow login with Claims Ops role
- Access to member's insurance policy details in CareBridge
- Updated fee schedule (updated quarterly, last refresh: Q3 2024)

## Step-by-Step Process

### 1. Claim Receipt & Initial Validation

When a claim arrives via EDI or manual upload, it automatically lands in the **Intake Queue** dashboard. Claims appear with status "Received."

- Verify claim ID is unique (system prevents duplicates automatically)
- Check that provider NPI, member ID, and date of service are present
- If any field is missing, move to "Pending Information" and notify submitter via email

### 2. Eligibility Verification

- Click the member ID to pull up their active policy in CareBridge
- Confirm policy was active on the date of service (DOS)
- Check for any coverage exclusions or waiting periods
- If ineligible, reject claim with reason code "ELG-001" and notify member within 2 business days

### 3. Benefit Determination

- Navigate to the **Benefits Calculator** tab
- Input procedure code (CPT or HCPCS), DOS, and provider type
- System automatically applies deductible, copay, and coinsurance based on member's plan tier
- Review calculated member responsibility and insurance liability

**Example:** CPT 99213 (office visit) under Silver Plan = $40 copay + 20% coinsurance on remaining balance.

### 4. Adjudication

- Click "Adjudicate" button to apply fee schedule
- System compares billed amount against contracted rate (usually 80-120% of Medicare)
- Any variance >15% triggers automatic hold for manual review
- Assuming no flags, system calculates final approval amount

### 5. Claim Decision & Notification

Select one of three outcomes:

- **Approve:** Claim moves to payment queue; generates EOB within 24 hours
- **Deny:** Requires reason code (e.g., "NOT-CVG" for non-covered service); member receives denial notice
- **Request More Info:** Suspends claim; sends secure message to provider

### 6. Payment Processing

Approved claims move to **Payment Batch** status. Batches process nightly at 11 PM ET.

- Payments typically reach provider bank accounts within 2-3 business days
- Check TimeTrax for payment confirmation logs (available under Reports > Payment History)

## Common Issues

- **"Duplicate claim detected"** – Check if member already has claim for same DOS/provider/amount in last 30 days
- **Fee schedule mismatch** – Verify provider contract type in CareBridge; update if needed
- **Stuck in adjudication** – High-variance claims often require Claims Ops supervisor approval (Slack @claims-supervisors)

## Escalation

For claims exceeding $50K or involving workers' compensation, contact Data & Analytics team before adjudication.
