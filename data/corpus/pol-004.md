# Remote Work and Hybrid Schedule Policy

**Effective Date:** January 15, 2023  
**Last Updated:** March 2024  
**Policy Owner:** HR Department

## Overview

Meridian Health Partners supports flexible work arrangements for eligible employees. This policy outlines guidelines for remote work and hybrid schedules across our 900-person organization.

## Eligibility

All full-time employees are eligible for remote or hybrid work arrangements, with the following exceptions:

- Claims Ops staff processing high-volume claims must maintain at least 3 days/week in-office
- Care Coordination team members require minimum 2 days/week on-site for patient-facing interactions
- Platform Engineering may work fully remote with manager approval
- Data & Analytics roles are flexible (typically 1-2 days on-site)

Contractors and temporary employees must confirm remote eligibility with HR before arrangement begins.

## Hybrid Schedule Options

**Option A (2-3 Days On-Site):** Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday in office. Monday and Friday flexible.

**Option B (1-2 Days On-Site):** Designated days determined by team lead. Must align with core collaboration hours (Tuesday-Thursday 10am-3pm ET).

**Full Remote:** Available for Data & Analytics, Platform Engineering, and select HR roles. Requires VP-level approval and 6-month probationary period.

## Work-From-Home Guidelines

**Equipment:** Meridian provides laptop, monitor, and keyboard. Internet stipend of $50/month for home office setup (submit receipt to Workday).

**VPN & Security:** All remote work requires VPN connection. Never access ClaimsFlow, CareBridge, or PulseBoard on public networks.

**Workspace:** Maintain a quiet, professional environment for video calls. No background video calls from vehicles or public spaces.

**Availability:** Remote employees must maintain posted office hours in Slack and respond to messages within 2 hours during business hours (8am-6pm ET).

## Scheduling and Approval

1. Discuss arrangement with direct manager
2. Complete Remote Work Agreement form (Confluence template: HR/Remote-Work-Forms)
3. Submit to HR via Workday by the 15th of prior month
4. Approval typically within 5 business days

Changes to schedule require 2 weeks' notice. Emergency schedule changes must be approved by team lead same day.

## Collaboration Expectations

- Attend all mandatory all-hands meetings (currently Tuesdays 2pm ET)
- Core hours: 10am-3pm ET, Monday-Friday
- Participate in team standup meetings (see Jira calendar for times)
- Quarterly in-person team meetings required for all remote staff

## Equipment and Reimbursement

Submit expense reports through Workday within 30 days. Approved items:

- Monitor/keyboard/mouse (up to $300)
- Ergonomic chair (up to $400)
- Internet stipend: $50/month
- Phone stipend: $30/month (remote only)

## Policy Changes

This policy is subject to revision. Updates will be announced via Slack and posted to Confluence. Questions should be directed to HR@meridianhealth.com or your People Operations partner.
