# Security and Data Handling Policy

**Last Updated:** March 2024 | **Owner:** Platform Engineering

## Overview

All Meridian Health Partners employees must follow these security and data handling practices. This applies to work with ClaimsFlow, CareBridge, PulseBoard, TimeTrax, and any patient or claims data.

## Access Control

- Request system access through Jira ticket to Platform Engineering (use project key `INFRA-ACCESS`)
- Access is role-based. Claims Ops staff get ClaimsFlow + read-only PulseBoard. Care Coordination gets CareBridge only.
- Managers approve access requests within 3 business days
- Quarterly access reviews happen in January, April, July, and October. Confirm your access is still needed.
- Terminate access within 24 hours of employee departure (HR notifies Platform Engineering via Slack #offboarding channel)

## Password and Authentication

- All system passwords must be 12+ characters with uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols
- Store passwords in 1Password (company vault). Do not use personal password managers.
- Enable multi-factor authentication (MFA) on all accounts. Use authenticator apps, not SMS when possible.
- Change passwords every 90 days. Set calendar reminders.
- Never share credentials. If you need to give someone temporary access, request it formally—don't share your login.

## Patient Data Handling

- Patient data includes names, medical record numbers, claim IDs, and health information in CareBridge
- Only access patient data needed for your job. Claims Ops doesn't need full medical histories; use claim summaries.
- Do not download patient data to personal devices or non-company laptops
- Screenshots containing patient data require approval from your manager and Data & Analytics. File requests in Confluence space `DATA-REQUESTS`
- Talking about specific patients outside work is prohibited, even with family members

## Remote Work and Devices

- Use company laptops only. Personal devices cannot access ClaimsFlow or CareBridge.
- Enable full disk encryption on your laptop (IT sets this up during onboarding)
- Lock your screen (Windows key + L) when stepping away, even for 2 minutes
- Use company VPN when working from coffee shops, airports, or home. VPN setup instructions are in Confluence under `IT-SETUP`
- Report lost or stolen laptops to IT immediately at security@meridianhealth.local

## Incident Reporting

- If you suspect a security breach, unauthorized access, or mishandled data, report it immediately to Platform Engineering in Slack (#security-incidents) or email security@meridianhealth.local
- Do not investigate on your own or tell colleagues before reporting
- Include: what happened, when, which systems/data, and who was involved
- Reports are treated confidentially

## Training

- Complete security training annually (assigned in Workday, due by December 31)
- New hires must complete training within first 30 days
- Care Coordination staff take additional HIPAA training every 18 months

## Questions?

Post in Slack #security-help or email security@meridianhealth.local. Platform Engineering responds within 1 business day.
