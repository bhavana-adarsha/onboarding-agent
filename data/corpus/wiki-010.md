# Slack Channel Guide

## Overview

This guide explains which Slack channels to use for different purposes at Meridian Health Partners. Use this to find the right place for questions, announcements, and collaboration.

## Company-Wide Channels

**#general** — Company announcements, all-hands updates, and major news. HR posts benefits reminders and policy changes here. Don't use for project work.

**#announcements** — Official statements only. Used by executive leadership and HR. Read-only for most employees.

**#random** — Non-work discussion, photos, memes, birthday shoutouts. Check here for office events and potluck signups.

**#help** — General questions about company systems, benefits, or policies. HR and admin staff monitor this daily. Response time: 24-48 hours.

## Product & Engineering

**#claimsflow-dev** — ClaimsFlow development, bug reports, and deployment updates. Platform Engineering and Claims Ops collaborate here. Post error logs and testing results.

**#carebridge-incidents** — Critical CareBridge issues only. Use this when the platform is down or affecting patient care. Tag @carebridge-oncall.

**#platform-engineering** — General infrastructure, Workday integrations, and GitHub discussions. Daily standup notes posted at 9:30am.

**#pulseBoard-questions** — Dashboard bugs, metric requests, and training. Data & Analytics owns this. Post screenshots of errors.

**#timetrax-support** — TimeTrax scheduling issues, timesheet problems, and payroll questions. HR responds within 4 hours during business days.

## Operational Teams

**#claims-ops-team** — Daily Claims Operations work, SLA tracking, and process improvements. Team lead posts daily metrics at 8am.

**#care-coordination-hub** — Care Coordination team updates, patient case discussions (anonymized), and workflow changes. Monitored by Care Coordination Manager.

**#data-analytics-requests** — Ad-hoc reporting requests for any team. Include context and deadline. Data & Analytics reviews Tuesdays and Thursdays.

**#hr-updates** — HR policy changes, hiring announcements, and compliance notices. Subscribe if you're a team lead.

## Project & Cross-Functional

**#claims-modernization-2024** — Q2-Q4 2024 project for ClaimsFlow upgrade. Includes Claims Ops, Platform Engineering, and Data & Analytics. Archived after December 31, 2024.

**#carebridge-expansion** — CareBridge rollout to new regional offices (Austin, Denver). Care Coordination and Platform Engineering. Active through March 2025.

**#all-hands-prep** — Organizing quarterly all-hands meetings. Anyone can volunteer. Next meeting: February 15.

## Guidelines

- Use threads to keep conversations organized
- Search existing channels before creating new ones
- Archive personal project channels after completion
- Don't use DMs for work decisions—post in channels for transparency

**Questions about this guide?** Ask in #help or contact your team lead.
