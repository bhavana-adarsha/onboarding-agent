# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy Update – Effective January 1, 2025

## Overview

Meridian Health Partners is updating the company-wide PTO policy effective January 1, 2025. All employees now receive **20 days of paid time off per year**, regardless of tenure or role. This supersedes the 2024 PTO policy and applies to all full-time employees across all departments.

## PTO Allocation

**Annual Entitlement:** 20 days per calendar year (160 hours for full-time employees)

- Days are allocated on January 1st each year
- Part-time employees receive prorated PTO based on their scheduled hours
- New hires receive prorated PTO based on their start date (calculated as: 20 days × months remaining in calendar year ÷ 12)

*Example: An employee starting March 15, 2025 receives approximately 10.5 days for 2025.*

## Requesting and Approval

1. **Submit requests in Workday** under Time Off → PTO Request minimum 2 weeks in advance
2. **Notify your direct manager** via Slack or email for same-week requests (not guaranteed approval)
3. **Managers must approve/deny within 48 hours** of submission
4. Cross-team coverage coordination is the responsibility of the requesting employee's manager

For Claims Ops and Care Coordination teams, please coordinate with your team leads to ensure adequate staffing. Contact your department head if conflicts arise.

## Carryover and Payout

- **No carryover permitted** – unused PTO does not roll into the following year
- Employees must use their 20 days within the calendar year (January 1 – December 31)
- Upon termination, employees receive payout for unused PTO at their current rate of pay
- Payout is processed through Workday payroll within the final paycheck

## Sick Leave

Sick leave is **separate from PTO** and is managed independently. Sick leave requests should be submitted in Workday and communicated to your manager as soon as possible (ideally before your shift).

## Holidays

Company holidays (11 days annually) are **separate from PTO**. See the 2025 Holiday Calendar posted in Confluence by HR.

## Remote and Hybrid Considerations

Employees working remotely or on hybrid schedules follow the same PTO policy. Out-of-office status must be set in Slack and your Workday calendar.

## Questions and Support

- **HR:** Contact hr@meridianhealth.com or post in #hr-general on Slack
- **Workday issues:** Submit tickets to the IT Help Desk via #platform-support
- **Manager questions:** Escalate to your department head

## Policy Effective Date

This policy takes effect **January 1, 2025** and replaces all prior PTO policies from 2024 and earlier.
