# Equipment and Laptop Policy for New Hires

## Overview

All new hires at Meridian Health Partners receive standard equipment to perform their role. This document outlines the equipment allocation process, acceptable use, and return procedures.

## Standard Equipment Package

### All Roles
- 1x laptop (see Laptop Specifications below)
- 1x monitor (Dell UltraSharp 24")
- 1x keyboard and mouse
- 1x docking station (where applicable)
- 1x phone (iPhone 13 or Android equivalent)
- Phone stipend: $50/month for personal device use (optional alternative)

### Additional Equipment by Team
- **Claims Ops**: Receipt scanner (Fujitsu ScanSnap iX1600)
- **Care Coordination**: Headset (Plantronics Voyager Focus UC)
- **Data & Analytics**: Secondary monitor (Dell UltraSharp 24")
- **Platform Engineering**: Secondary monitor + mechanical keyboard (employee choice)

## Laptop Specifications

All new hires receive a MacBook Pro (M2, 16GB RAM, 512GB SSD) or Dell XPS 15 (Intel i7, 16GB RAM, 512GB SSD) based on role and team preference.

**Requested at:** Platform Engineering via Jira ticket (Equipment Request template)  
**Provisioned by:** Day 1 of employment  
**Managed by:** IT Service Portal in Workday

## Ordering Process

1. HR submits equipment request in Workday at least 10 business days before start date
2. Platform Engineering approves request and orders equipment
3. Equipment is imaged and ready before new hire's first day
4. New hire signs Equipment Acknowledgment Form (template in Confluence under IT Policies)
5. Equipment is delivered to new hire's desk or mailed if remote

## Acceptable Use

- Equipment is for business purposes only
- Personal use is permitted for incidental activities during breaks
- All equipment must comply with Meridian Health Partners security policies
- Passwords must be changed within 24 hours of first login
- Two-factor authentication is mandatory for all devices
- Remote work requires VPN connection for access to ClaimsFlow, CareBridge, and PulseBoard

## Care and Maintenance

- Employees are responsible for physical care of equipment
- Report damage or malfunction to Platform Engineering immediately via IT Service Portal
- Standard wear and tear is covered; intentional damage may result in replacement costs
- Laptops should be backed up weekly via company OneDrive (automatic for most roles)
- Do not attempt to repair equipment yourself

## Equipment Return

Upon separation or role change:

1. Employee returns all equipment to HR within 5 business days
2. Laptop data is remotely wiped by Platform Engineering
3. Employee signs Equipment Return Confirmation in Workday
4. Equipment is refurbished or recycled per company standards
5. Employee is not reimbursed for unreturned equipment; costs may be deducted from final paycheck

## Questions or Issues

Contact Platform Engineering via Slack (#it-support) or submit a ticket in the IT Service Portal. Response time: 2 business hours during standard hours (8 AM–5 PM EST, Monday–Friday).
