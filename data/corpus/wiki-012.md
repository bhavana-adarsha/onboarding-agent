# Tips for New Employees at Meridian Health Partners

Welcome to Meridian! This page has tips to help you get started. Check back often—it gets updated as we learn what actually helps.

## Your First Week

**Monday morning:** HR will send you links to Workday (payroll/benefits), GitHub (code repos), and our Slack workspace. Join #general and #new-employees. 

**Day 2-3:** Meet your team lead. Ask them about:
- Your specific onboarding buddy (usually assigned by HR)
- Which app you'll mainly use (ClaimsFlow, CareBridge, PulseBoard, or TimeTrax)
- Your first real task (usually small, non-critical)

**By Friday:** Complete mandatory training modules in Workday (takes ~2 hours). You'll need screenshots for HR.

## Getting Around

**Jira & Confluence:** All projects live here. If you can't find documentation on a process, search Confluence first. If nothing exists, ask in Slack—don't guess.

**Slack channels:**
- #claims-ops for Claims Operations questions
- #care-coord for Care Coordination team
- #data-analytics for reporting/dashboards
- #platform-eng for technical issues
- #random for memes (yes, really)

**The office:** Building 2, 3rd floor is Claims Ops. Building 1 is where Care Coordination and Data & Analytics sit. Platform Engineering is remote-first but has desks on 2nd floor if needed.

## App-Specific Notes

**ClaimsFlow:** Handles ~15,000 claims monthly. Your manager will walk you through the UI. Common issue: date fields use MM/DD/YYYY format. Don't use slashes—use dashes or it rejects.

**CareBridge:** Slower than you'd expect on Mondays. Restart your browser if it freezes. Not ideal, but we're working on it (see Platform Engineering backlog, item MHP-3847).

**PulseBoard:** Updated nightly, not real-time. Don't refresh obsessively.

**TimeTrax:** Submit timesheets every Friday by 3 PM. Payroll runs Tuesday. Late submissions delay your check.

## Random But Important

- **Passwords:** Change yours after first login. IT requires updates every 90 days.
- **Expenses:** Use Workday. Keep receipts for 6 months. Reimbursement takes 10-14 business days.
- **Meetings:** Most are on Teams or Zoom. Calendar invites usually include links.
- **Questions:** Your team lead > Slack channel > Confluence. In that order.

## Lunch & Culture

Cafeteria in Building 1 is decent. Tuesdays are usually taco day. Microwaves in both buildings. 

We do team lunches quarterly. Claims Ops did one in March at Riverside Grill—pretty good.

## After Your First Month

Schedule a 1:1 with your manager to discuss:
- What's confusing?
- What's working?
- What do you need to succeed?

You should feel reasonably comfortable by week 4. If not, speak up—we want you here.
