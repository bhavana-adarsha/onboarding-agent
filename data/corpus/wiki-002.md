# How a Claim Moves Through ClaimsFlow: End-to-End

## Overview

ClaimsFlow processes all incoming insurance claims for our partner networks. A typical claim takes 8-12 business days from submission to either approval or denial. This document outlines each stage.

## Stage 1: Intake & Initial Validation (Day 1)

Claims arrive via three channels: EDI feeds from partner systems, our web portal, or manual uploads. The system performs basic validation:

- Required fields present (member ID, provider NPI, DOS, service code)
- Date of service not older than 180 days
- Member exists in our database
- Duplicate detection against last 90 days

Invalid claims are rejected with specific error codes and marked "INTAKE_FAILED" in ClaimsFlow. Valid claims move to the queue and are assigned a Claim ID (format: CLM-YYYYMM-#####).

## Stage 2: Pre-Adjudication Rules Engine (Day 2-3)

Our rules engine runs ~47 automated checks, including:

- Benefit coverage verification (checking CareBridge for active enrollment)
- Network provider validation
- Bundling rules (e.g., lab panels must bundle under one code)
- Frequency limits (e.g., preventive screenings per calendar year)
- Age/gender edits (pediatric services for adults, etc.)

Claims that pass all rules are marked "RULES_CLEARED." Those with violations are flagged for Claims Ops review or auto-denied based on severity.

## Stage 3: Manual Review (Day 4-6)

Complex claims requiring human judgment go to Claims Ops team. Our current SLA is 2 business days per claim. Reviewers use the ClaimsFlow dashboard to:

- Pull member eligibility from Workday integration
- Review clinical documentation attachments
- Compare against plan-specific policies (stored in Confluence)
- Consult with Care Coordination via CareBridge if needed

Reviewers either approve, deny, or request more information from the provider.

## Stage 4: Determination & Communication (Day 7-10)

Approved claims are priced using our fee schedule and sent to accounting. Denials are logged with reason codes (e.g., "NOT_COVERED," "FREQUENCY_EXCEEDED"). Both are communicated to providers via EDI or email.

Members receive an Explanation of Benefits (EOB) generated by PulseBoard and mailed within 5 business days.

## Stage 5: Payment & Closure (Day 11-12)

Approved claims are batched and paid via ACH transfer (typically 2-3 day settlement). Claims are marked "PAID" and archived in ClaimsFlow.

Denied or disputed claims remain in "OPEN" status for up to 60 days to allow appeals.

## Key Metrics (Tracked in PulseBoard)

- First-pass approval rate: 78% (target: 85%)
- Average processing time: 9.3 days
- Denial rate: 12% (mostly frequency/coverage issues)
