# Manager Runbook: 90-Day New Hire Performance Review

## Overview

All new hires at Meridian Health Partners receive a formal performance review at the 90-day mark. This runbook covers the review process, documentation requirements, and compensation adjustment procedures for managers.

## Timeline & Scheduling

Schedule the review for the employee's 90-day anniversary (±3 business days is acceptable). Send a calendar invite at least one week in advance to the employee and include your direct report's skip-level manager as an observer.

**Key dates to track:**
- Day 1: New hire start date (recorded in Workday)
- Day 60: Informal check-in (manager discretion)
- Day 90: Formal review meeting
- Day 95: All documentation due in Workday

## Pre-Review Preparation

1. **Gather feedback** from the employee's direct teammates and any cross-functional collaborators using the "90-Day Feedback Form" in Confluence (search: `90-day-feedback-template`). Request feedback at least 5 business days before the review.

2. **Review onboarding checklist** in Workday. Confirm the employee has completed all required training modules (Claims Ops staff should have ClaimsFlow certification; Care Coordination hires need CareBridge training; all staff complete Compliance 101).

3. **Assess job performance** against the role description and initial 90-day goals set during onboarding. Document specific examples—both strengths and development areas.

4. **Check system access.** Verify the employee has appropriate permissions in ClaimsFlow, CareBridge, PulseBoard, and TimeTrax per their role. Escalate access issues to Platform Engineering (Slack: #platform-eng-support).

## Conducting the Review Meeting

Schedule 60 minutes. Follow this structure:

- **Introduction (5 min):** Explain the review's purpose and that this is a two-way conversation.
- **Performance discussion (30 min):** Cover onboarding experience, technical skill development, teamwork, and alignment with company values.
- **Employee input (15 min):** Ask what's working well, what's challenging, and what support they need.
- **Next steps (10 min):** Discuss career path, any compensation adjustments (see section below), and 90+ day goals.

Document notes in Workday's review field. If performance is below expectations, loop in HR (Slack: #hr-support) before finalizing.

## Compensation Adjustments

Employees performing above expectations may be eligible for a salary adjustment or bonus.

**To initiate a compensation change:**

1. Access the "Compensation Adjustment Form" via Workday → Admin Panel → Compensation Management (requires manager role access; if you can't find it, contact Workday admin in #platform-eng-support).

2. Complete all required fields:
   - Employee ID and name
   - Current salary
   - Proposed new salary or bonus amount
   - Business justification (2-3 sentences explaining performance highlights)
   - Effective date (typically the 91st day)

3. Submit for approval to your skip-level manager and the HR Business Partner assigned to your department.

4. HR will confirm approval within 5 business days and process in Workday.

## Documentation & Closeout

- Upload the completed review form to Workday by Day 95.
- Send a summary email to the employee confirming compensation decisions and next-step goals.
- Archive your feedback notes in Confluence under your team's 90-day reviews folder.
