# TimeTrax Basics

## Overview

TimeTrax is our scheduling and timesheet management system used by all 900 staff members at Meridian Health Partners. It integrates with Workday for payroll processing and is maintained by Platform Engineering.

## Timesheets

### Submitting Your Timesheet

Employees enter their hours in TimeTrax by end of day Friday each week. The system operates on a standard Monday-Sunday week cycle.

**Steps:**
1. Log in at timetrax.meridian.local using your SSO credentials
2. Click "My Timesheets" in the left navigation
3. Enter hours for each day (breakdown by project/app if applicable)
4. Add any notes in the "Comments" field (e.g., "Holiday on Monday," "Remote work")
5. Click "Submit for Approval" by 5 PM Friday

**Important:** Timesheets submitted after Friday 5 PM will be flagged as late and may delay payroll processing by one cycle.

### Corrections

If you need to modify a submitted timesheet, contact your direct manager or your team's TimeTrax admin. Do not resubmit without approval. Corrections must be made by the following Wednesday to be included in that pay period.

## Schedules

### Viewing Your Schedule

Your shift schedule is visible under the "My Schedule" tab. Schedules are published monthly by the 15th of the preceding month.

- **Claims Ops:** Primarily 8 AM–5 PM, Monday–Friday (some weekend coverage)
- **Care Coordination:** Rotating shifts, including evenings until 8 PM
- **Data & Analytics:** Standard 8 AM–5 PM
- **Platform Engineering:** Flexible hours; core hours 10 AM–3 PM

### Requesting Schedule Changes

Use the "Request Shift Swap" feature to trade shifts with colleagues. Both parties must approve the swap, then submit to your manager for final sign-off. Swaps must be requested at least 7 days in advance.

For extended schedule changes (recurring), email your team's HR Business Partner listed on the [HR Contacts](https://confluence.meridian.local/display/HR/Contacts) Confluence page.

## Approvals

### Manager Approval Workflow

Managers review submitted timesheets in TimeTrax under "Pending Approvals." The system shows:
- Employee name and submitted hours
- Any flagged discrepancies (e.g., over 40 hours without OT pre-approval)
- Previous week's hours for reference

**Approval deadline:** By 5 PM Wednesday following the Friday submission.

Unapproved timesheets are automatically escalated to skip-level managers if not actioned by Wednesday EOD.

### PTO and Leave

Paid time off (vacation, sick, personal days) must be requested in Workday, not TimeTrax. TimeTrax will auto-populate approved PTO once Workday syncs (usually within 2 hours). If PTO doesn't appear in TimeTrax, contact the HR team via Slack (#hr-support).

## Troubleshooting

**Can't log in?** Verify your SSO is active in Workday or reach out to #platform-engineering-support.

**Hours not syncing to Workday?** Wait 24 hours after manager approval. Escalate in #timetrax-issues if still missing.

**Questions?** Check the TimeTrax FAQ on Confluence or ask your team's admin.
