# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Policy Overview

Meridian Health Partners reimburses reasonable business expenses incurred by employees in the course of their work. All reimbursement requests must be submitted within 30 days of expense date. Expenses submitted after 90 days will not be reimbursed.

## Expense Categories and Limits

### Travel

- **Airfare**: Coach/economy class only. Exceptions require VP approval.
- **Mileage**: $0.67 per mile (IRS standard rate). Use Workday mileage calculator.
- **Rental cars**: Mid-size or smaller. Weekly rates preferred over daily.
- **Lodging**: $150/night limit (major metros: $200/night). Includes taxes.
- **Meals while traveling**: $60/day limit. Alcoholic beverages capped at $15 per meal.

### Office & Professional

- **Software/tools**: Pre-approval required for purchases over $500. Contact Platform Engineering.
- **Conference registration**: Up to $2,500 without approval. Above $2,500 requires director sign-off.
- **Professional development**: Books, courses, certifications up to $1,000/year per employee.
- **Office supplies**: Coordinate through HR. Personal reimbursement only if urgent.

### Client/Business Meals

- **Client entertainment**: $75/person limit. Requires business purpose documentation.
- **Team meals**: $30/person limit for working lunches. Requires attendee list.

## Submission Process

1. **Collect receipts**: Keep original receipts for all expenses over $25. Digital copies acceptable (photograph or PDF).
2. **Log in Workday**: Enter expense details under "Reimbursement Requests" module. Attach receipt images.
3. **Add business purpose**: One-sentence explanation of business purpose required for all expenses.
4. **Manager approval**: Submit to direct manager for review and approval in Workday.
5. **Finance processing**: After manager approval, Finance team processes within 5-7 business days.
6. **Payment**: Direct deposit to employee's registered bank account (same as payroll).

## Timeline Requirements

- **Submission deadline**: 30 days from expense date
- **Manager review SLA**: 3 business days
- **Finance processing**: 5-7 business days after manager approval
- **Payment window**: 10-14 business days from original submission

*Note: Expenses submitted 31-90 days after incurrence may be reimbursed at manager's discretion but are not guaranteed.*

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses

- Personal items (clothing, toiletries, gym memberships)
- Commuting to/from primary work location
- Traffic violations, parking tickets, or vehicle damage
- Home internet or phone bills (unless explicitly approved as remote work equipment)
- Alcoholic beverages for non-client meals
- Childcare or pet care

## Special Approvals

Expenses exceeding stated limits require pre-approval from your department director. Email requests to your director and CC finance@meridianhealth.com with business justification.

**Questions?** Contact HR at hr@meridianhealth.com or Finance at reimbursement@meridianhealth.com. Most FAQs are documented in Confluence under "Finance Policies."
